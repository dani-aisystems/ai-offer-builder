## Client context (Phase 1)

- **Business:** licensed electrical contractor in the Greater Toronto Area. Residential and commercial work, panel upgrades, EV charger installation and emergency service.
- **Market:** local, B2C homeowners plus B2B general contractors and property managers.
- **What sets them apart:** licensed electricians, fast response and transparent estimates.
- **Today:** a basic, outdated website that brings in very few qualified leads. Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram are in place. Work comes from referrals, Google Maps, repeat customers, general contractors and phone calls.

## Approved packages (as approved in chat, full lists)

### Package 1 — Foundation (CA$1,450 one-time · before HST, plus applicable tax)
**Single-page site**

- **Custom single-page design** — Example Electrical looks as professional online as its work on site.
- **Mobile-first, fast-loading build** — the site works for someone on a phone in a basement with one bar of signal.
- **Always-visible tap-to-call button** — someone with an electrical emergency reaches you in one tap.
- **Complete services overview** — residential, commercial, panel upgrades, EV chargers and emergency service at a glance.
- **Credentials section** — your ECRA/ESA licence number and licensed electricians up front, meeting ESA's website requirement.
- **Quote request form to your inbox** — after-hours inquiries are captured instead of lost.
- **Service area and Google reviews** — visitors see you cover their community, and that customers vouch for you.
- **Basic on-page SEO** — page title, description and headings set up so Google understands what you do and where.

**Support:** first month free, then CA$89/month. Covers hosting, security updates, daily backups, uptime monitoring and small text updates.

### Package 2 — Growth (CA$2,650 one-time · before HST, plus applicable tax) — RECOMMENDED
**Multi-page site, up to 10 pages · everything in Foundation, plus:**

- **A page for every service** — each service speaks to its own customer and can rank for its own searches.
- **Smart quote form with a commercial path** — job type, urgency and photos for faster estimates, plus a direct route for general contractors.
- **Project gallery** — before-and-after photos make the quality of your workmanship visible.
- **Permits and ESA inspections FAQ** — answers homeowners' biggest worries and gives search engines and AI tools clear answers.
- **Google Business Profile optimization** — categories, services and photos linked to matching pages, for stronger Maps visibility.
- **Call and form tracking** — a monthly lead report shows which pages bring in calls and quote requests.
- **Switchable emergency banner** — an after-hours banner with the emergency number that Example Electrical can turn on or off themselves.
- **On-page SEO for every page** — titles, descriptions and headings written for each service's own searches.

Why Growth fits Example Electrical: every service gets its own page and every inquiry is qualified, with no ad spend needed yet.

**Support:** first month free, then CA$149/month. Everything in Foundation's support, plus occasional content updates and the monthly lead report.

### Package 3 — Dominance (CA$4,850 one-time · before HST, plus applicable tax)
**Multi-page site, up to 15 pages, plus Google Ads and local SEO · everything in Growth, plus:**

- **Google search ads with call buttons** — people ready to hire can call you straight from Google.
- **Ongoing ad management and tracking** — weekly adjustments, with every dollar tied to real calls and inquiries.
- **Two dedicated ad landing pages** — built so paid clicks turn into calls, for example for emergency service and EV chargers.
- **GTA community pages** — be found in each community you want more work from.
- **Local SEO and Canadian directory listings** — consistent listings and monthly SEO work strengthen your Google Maps position.
- **Two local guides a month** — helpful articles like "Do I need an ESA permit for a panel upgrade?" keep bringing in search traffic.

**Support:** first month free, then CA$1,190/month. Covers hosting and maintenance, content updates, Google Ads management and ongoing local SEO. Ad spend is billed separately and paid directly to Google.
